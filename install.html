Put Spoonbook on
your home screen.

Spoonbook isn’t in the app stores. Instead you add it from your browser, which takes about ten seconds. Then it opens from its own icon, full screen, and works with no signal.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open spoonbook.co.uk in Safari. (In other browsers on iPhone, look for the same Share button.)
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen, or the top on iPad.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Spoonbook is now on your home screen.

Android

  1. Open spoonbook.co.uk in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install if Spoonbook offers it, or tap the menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then Install.

Once installed, you can share recipes straight into Spoonbook from Chrome and other apps.

Laptop or computer

  1. Chrome or Edge: open spoonbook.co.uk and click the install icon at the right of the address bar (a small screen with an arrow), then Install.
  2. Safari on a Mac: open spoonbook.co.uk, then choose File → Add to Dock.

You can also just use Spoonbook in a browser tab. Everything works the same.

Not working?

Make sure you’re using the browser named above, and that you’ve opened spoonbook.co.uk rather than a link inside another app (such as Facebook or WhatsApp). If it opened inside another app, use its menu to Open in browser first. Still stuck? Email hello@spoonbook.co.uk.
