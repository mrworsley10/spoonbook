What it does

👨‍👩‍👧

Cook together

Invite family to your cookbook. Everyone can add recipes, or you can let someone just look and copy.

🔗

Save from anywhere

Paste a link from a recipe website, snap a page of a cookbook, or bring across old emails and other apps.

📅

Plan the week

Pick dinners for each day, or tap Fill my week and Spoonbook suggests them from your own recipes.

🛒

Shop together

One tap adds a recipe’s ingredients to the list, sorted by aisle. Tick things off and everyone sees it.

👩‍🍳

Cook hands-free

Big clear steps, timers you can hear, and it can read each step aloud.

📶

Works with no signal

Your cookbook is saved on your phone, so it opens in the supermarket or a kitchen with poor wifi.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no adverts and nothing to pay. If paid extras are ever added, you’ll be told in the app first, and you can always download every recipe you’ve saved.

Is it in the App Store or Google Play?

Not yet. Spoonbook is a web app: you open spoonbook.co.uk and add it to your home screen, and from then on it opens like any other app. It takes ten seconds. See how.

Who can see my recipes?

Only the people you invite to your cookbook. If you send someone a link to a single recipe, anyone with that link can see that one recipe, and nothing else.

Can my family share one cookbook?

Yes, that’s what it’s for. Send an invite from the app. Choose whether they can add and change recipes, or only look and copy them. You can belong to more than one cookbook too, for example your own and your parents’.

Can I bring my recipes from another app?

Yes. Spoonbook can read links from most recipe websites, photos of printed recipes, saved emails and whole email folders, and exports from apps such as ChefTap.

Does it use AI?

No. Reading recipes, sorting the shopping list, suggesting the week’s dinners and working out calories all happen on your phone using ordinary rules, not AI.

Can I trust the allergy and calorie information?

They’re a helpful guide worked out from the ingredients as written, not a guarantee. Always check labels if someone has an allergy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap ⋯ then Your account, then Delete my account. It’s deleted straight away. You can also email hello@spoonbook.co.uk.
